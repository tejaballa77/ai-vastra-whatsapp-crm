# 🚀 AI Vastra — WhatsApp Web CRM Overlay (Chrome Extension)

This Chrome Extension injects a **CRM Lead Drawer, Auto-Responder & AI Smart Reply System** directly inside official WhatsApp Web (`https://web.whatsapp.com`).

---

## 🛠️ Step-by-Step Installation Guide (30 Seconds)

### Step 1: Open Chrome Extensions
1. Open Google Chrome on your computer.
2. In the browser address bar, type: **`chrome://extensions`** and press **Enter**.

---

### Step 2: Enable Developer Mode
1. In the top-right corner of the Extensions page, toggle the **Developer mode** switch to **ON**.

---

### Step 3: Load Unpacked Extension
1. Click the **Load unpacked** button in the top-left corner.
2. Select the folder:
   `C:\Users\mobee\Downloads\ai-whatsapp-crm\chrome_extension`
3. Click **Select Folder**.

---

### Step 4: Open WhatsApp Web
1. Go to **`https://web.whatsapp.com`**.
2. Look at the bottom-right corner of your screen — you will see the floating **`✨ AI Vastra CRM`** button!
3. Click **`AI Vastra CRM`** to slide open your CRM Drawer directly next to any active WhatsApp chat!

---

## ✨ Features Included:
* 🟢 **Lead Status Tags**: Assign *Interested*, *Warm*, or *Not Interested* with 1-click.
* 📅 **Follow-up Date Scheduler**: Native date picker + quick `+1 Day`, `+3 Days`, `+7 Days` chips.
* 📝 **Auto-Saving Notes**: Textarea for customer garment preferences, catalog requests & sizes.
* 🚀 **1-Click Message Templates**: Insert proposal text & pricing directly into WhatsApp Web chat input.
* 🔒 **100% Native & Secure**: Runs inside official WhatsApp Web — zero logouts, zero QR refreshes, zero missing saved names or media!
